New Data Product

Client
A global entertainment, sports, and news company with +$5B in annual advertising revenue
Challenge
Disrupting a decades-old paradigm of TV campaign measurement and execution based on the notion of age and gender-based metrics
Strategy
Integrate enriched consumer datasets with traditional TV measures and develop a series of integrated forecasting and optimization models to allow the client to operationalize advanced advertising methodologies
Outcome

Alternative Data Licensing

Client
A major digital marketing company operating in the CPG retail and marketing space
Challenge
Finding incremental revenue by identifying alternative data monetization opportunities which do not compete with core business model
Strategy
Enrich retailer point-of-sale data in an aggregated and anonymized format, and build technology stack to enable data-feed licensing to end-clients
Outcome
$4M in incremental revenue in the first 12 months of project, and forecasted $10M in year two

+$120Mper year in ticketing revenue

Data Science & Operational Efficiency

Client
A major US-based airline with one of the largest global fleets
Challenge
Finding practical and efficient means for utilizing consumer preferences of flying on particular days of the week, times, and on particular airline brands to generate additional profit
Strategy
Leverage data from schedules, revenue accounting, and flight availability to build out consumer choice models to significantly improve and add flexibility to pricing models
Outcome
Operational implementation of new models yielded 1-5% increases in company ticketing revenue, depending on market, realizing +$120M per year
